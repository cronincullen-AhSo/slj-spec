Three things I'd build first

1

A show playbook

One repeatable file per Jam: countdown, cue sheet, budget. The team can run a night without you in every role.

2

Sponsor packages

Simple tiers, from a presenting partner down to the goodie bag. Asahi is a natural first conversation.

3

A post-show report

A handful of numbers within 72 hours of every show, so decisions about pricing, new dates and partners get easier each month.
